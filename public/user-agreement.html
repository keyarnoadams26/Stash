STASH User Agreement

Version 1.0 · Draft effective 25 November 2026

Draft notice: This document is a plain-language starting point for STASH testing. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch. The legal business name, contact details, age requirements and market-specific terms must be completed first.

1. Agreement to use STASH

By creating an account or using STASH, you acknowledge that you have read this User Agreement, the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not create an account or use the service.

2. What STASH does

STASH is designed to help users organise subscriptions, recurring payments, budgets and related spending information. Some features may be experimental, unavailable or still under development. The features actually available in your account are the features you can use.

3. Your account

  • Provide information that is accurate and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your sign-in details secure and do not share access without permission.
  • Tell STASH promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.
  • Use only accounts and information that you are authorised to access.

4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for checking subscription details, payment amounts and due dates against the relevant service provider. You must use STASH lawfully, respect other people's rights and not attempt to disrupt, reverse engineer or gain unauthorised access to the service.

5. No payment or cancellation on your behalf

Unless a feature is specifically introduced and clearly described as active, STASH does not make payments, change provider contracts or cancel subscriptions for you. Recording a subscription as cancelled in STASH does not cancel it with the provider. Contact the provider directly and check that the cancellation is confirmed.

6. Recommendations and savings

Recommendations, comparisons, reminders and calculations are provided for information and organisation. They may be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. STASH does not guarantee that an alternative is available or that you will save money. A savings-related fee or commission may only apply if it is clearly disclosed in advance, the saving has genuinely been achieved and verified, and you have separately agreed to the applicable charge.

7. Financial and bank information

STASH is not a bank, lender, payment service provider or financial adviser. Do not provide bank passwords to STASH. Any future bank connection must be separately described, use an identified provider and obtain required permissions before activation.

8. Plans and advertising

Plan features, prices, trials, renewal dates and cancellation conditions must be displayed before a purchase. A plan is not paid or a trial activated merely because it is shown in a demo or preview. Advertising may be displayed on the free plan if and when an advertising service is enabled, subject to the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

9. Privacy and data

STASH handles personal information as explained in its Privacy Policy. You should review that policy before using the service. Do not upload information about another person unless you have a lawful reason and authority to do so.

10. Availability and changes

Features may change, be interrupted or be withdrawn as STASH develops. We will take reasonable steps to maintain the service, but uninterrupted availability cannot be promised.

11. Suspension or closure

Access may be restricted where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate misuse, comply with law or secure the service. Account closure and data handling are also subject to the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy and applicable law.

12. Legal rights

This Agreement does not remove or limit any consumer, privacy or other legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply to the extent permitted by law.

13. Contact and legal identity

Before public launch, STASH must publish the responsible legal entity's full name, physical or registered address, support email and privacy contact. These details have intentionally not been invented for this draft.
