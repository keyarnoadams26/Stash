STASH Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 25 November 2026

Important: This is a starter policy, not legal advice or a complete compliance assessment. It must be reviewed by a privacy lawyer and updated to match the actual services, processors, retention periods and countries in which STASH operates.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, STASH may process account details such as your name and email address, subscription names and prices, payment dates, currency, app preferences, support messages, and technical or diagnostic information.

2. Bank and payment information

Bank connection is not currently active. If introduced, STASH must identify the bank-data provider, explain exactly what data is accessed, obtain any required permissions, and describe security, retention and deletion arrangements before enabling the feature. STASH should not collect or store bank passwords.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain STASH, display subscription and spending information, secure accounts, respond to support requests, improve the service and meet legal obligations. Advertising and analytics practices must be described accurately before those services are enabled.

4. Service providers

STASH may use third-party providers for hosting, authentication, databases, payments, analytics and advertising. Before launch, this policy must name or appropriately describe the actual providers and link to their privacy information.

5. Sharing

Personal information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service, where you direct us to do so, or where required or permitted by law. STASH does not sell personal information as a general business practice.

6. International transfers

STASH is intended for a global market. Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Appropriate safeguards and disclosures must be implemented where required by applicable law.

7. Security and retention

We will use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes, legal requirements and legitimate dispute handling.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Add a working privacy contact and process for submitting requests before launch.

9. Children

STASH is not designed for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. The business must set and enforce an appropriate minimum age for each market.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as STASH changes. The current version and effective date will be displayed on this page.

11. Contact

Before launch, add the responsible legal entity's name, privacy contact email, address and any required data-protection officer details here.
